# MyTube AI Release 1.5

## Added
- Smart Export supports landscape 16:9 videos and vertical 9:16 Shorts.
- Vertical exports crop to fill portrait dimensions instead of letterboxing.
- Watermark placement honors the saved corner/center setting.
- Smart Export accepts multiple audio tracks and mixes them in the final MP4.

## Requirements and limits
- FFmpeg must be installed on the private backend.
- Upload your own licensed/authorized video and audio assets.
- This release renders existing assets; it does not create AI video, images, speech, or music. Those still require configured compatible engines and models.
